Today I left camp a little late and had a 17.5 miles to do. Things started out great but the temp went into the 90s which made for a rough day of hiking. The terrain was challenging with some rock scrambling sections that required some thought on how to traverse that section with a 30+lb pack-steep with few hand and toe holds! I got to camp around 730pm and had a .4mile hike for water not what you want at the end of a tough day. So water-dinner- lights out by 10pm. Another great day on the trail!
