I was running slow today after our big push yesterday and I found climbing the mountains
difficult. At mid day I reached Grafton Notch trail head where there was trail magic-sodas,
cherries, candy, muffins and egg with bacon and cheese sandwiches. During my rest here I
met Rodney a shuttle guy I was going to call later in the day to arrange a shuttle ride to the
Hostel Human Nature a geodesic dome from East B Hill Rd. We met Rodney at 630 along with
three other hikers who asked to share the shuttle into Andover-it was a tight fit with 4 in the
back seat. We got to Andover which has one store/diner/gas station Mills Market where we
got snacks then onto the Hostel 15 miles outside of town. We got to the Hostel and found out
the owner was on the survival show “Alone and Afraid” multiple time in different location
around the world.
