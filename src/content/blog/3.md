Today I pushed to Woods Hole Shelter at 27.7 miles.  To camp at woods Hole you have to have a bear canister so their were only 7 people at the shelter.  We had a campfire and talked about the trail and we were fortunate to have Fire Brand here to keep the fire going.  Turned in at 830 to get up and head to Blood Mountain to catch SunRise.
