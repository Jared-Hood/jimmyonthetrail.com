I made my way to Delaware Water Gap and stayed at the local church hostel. I was able to get a shower but the bunk area was very musty since the shower was in the same space with no vent so I ended up sleeping on a chair wit an ottoman in the common area. The town had a great farmers bakery with great sandwiches and pies so I had dinner there. I also got a resupply here from the local sunoco/store. The town is also known for the Deer Head Inn where they have jazz players perform but unfortunately for me they had a private event scheduled.
