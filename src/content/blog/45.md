This morning I got underway at 6:50 for a 17.5 mile hike to the Weary Feet Hostel.  The morning was chilly but felt great to hike with a mid layer on as I made my way up the first hill for a 6 mile ridge walk before descending for 2 miles where I stopped for lunch at 10:30.  I completed my day with a 9.5 mile hike again with ascent then ridge and descent to my destination crossing my first hiker suspension bridge-it would sway a little with each step which definitely was a new feeling given all other bridges were fixed and stable.
