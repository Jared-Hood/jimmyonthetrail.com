I left the Hikers Hut slacking to a logging road 13 miles North crossing the Saddleback and Horn Mountains both over 4k with big ups and downs when I got to the logging road there was a small waterfall where I filled up with 4liters of water and walked 1.5miles to the parking area where our packs were being delivered so we could spend the night in the woods. Once at the parking area it was evident there was no water available so I shared it with Lazarus so he could prep dinner knowing we could fill in the morning back at the trail head.
