I left Weary Feet after breakfast scrambled eggs, sausage, pancakes and coffee-yum.  The walk today was easy and felt like I was on a path you would find in a city park-flat!  I stopped at the Woods Hole Hostel which is a log cabin home and bunk house constructed of chestnut logs back in the 1880’s timeframe.  They are very organic as most food comes from their own garden and meals are communal with yoga at 4 in the front yard.  They also make a great smoothie!
