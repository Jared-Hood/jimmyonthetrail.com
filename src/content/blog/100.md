Today was a lot of short up and down hill climbs with an end of the day stop at a lake. The lake had a concession stand where I continued the deli portion of the AT with food from the grill and ice cream then a short nap before hiking the last 4 miles to the shelter. Once at the RPH shelter we found that we could get pizza delivered to the road 200feet from the shelter so of course more food was ordered. Most of us commented that if only we knew about the available food on trail we wouldn’t have packed out as much food in our packs!
