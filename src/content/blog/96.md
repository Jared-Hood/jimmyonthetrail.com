I left Unionville to head to Warwick and stay at the Warwick Drive-in. It was a typical hike, ups and downs watching my feet so as not to trip on roots or rocks. I got to the Warwick Turnpike with BeastMode and was given a ride to the drive-in by a past AT hiker. The campsite at the Drive-in was packed with 12 tents and all the hikers were at the local beer garden eating and enjoying a few beers before the show-alcohol was not allow in our camp site. We watched Buzz Lightyear but mostly heard the Jurassic Park show thru all the car radios tuned to that show. Our portable radios had poor volume. As is typical we were all in our tents right after the show since it ended well after hiker midnight.
