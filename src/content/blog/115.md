Today was a good day with a steep decent into Manchester Center which is an expensive town. I stayed at the EconoMotel outside of town which required a $10 shuttle every where I needed to go. So into town for resupply and dinner with a 8 o’clock curfew for the last shuttle or walk 2 miles back to the motel. In town I meet up with Lazarus for dinner and we went over the hiking plan I put together that limited the daily miles below 18 miles with shorter days in the Whites of NH where the terrain gets steeper and we will have numerous mountains over 4000ft. We decided to meet the next day at the Big Branch Shelter
