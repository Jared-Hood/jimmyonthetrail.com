As planned our shuttle driver arrived at 7am to pickup our packs and we continued slacking 10 miles over Spaulding and Sugarloaf Mountains both 4k to another logging rd where we were picked up by a shuttle from the Hostel of Maine. The hostel is one of the nicest hostels on the trail-they have food,beer and wine available and do a nightly run to a local restaurant for dinner pickup. We arranged for another 2 days of slack packing with the HoM.
