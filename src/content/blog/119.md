We left the deli by 6:50 to catch the first bus back to the trail. Todays hike was tough as the trail went up and over Killington peak and the path was steep with a lot of exposed roots to navigate over. I took the side trail to the top of the Killington Ski Lift where there was a food truck and a lot of day trippers coming up for the view along with mountain bikers using the ski runs. I finished the day at The Lookout cabin for the night which has a roof top deck that provided great views. We shared the cabin with two other hikers for a quite night on the trail.
