I left the IronMasters Hostel an old mansion and headed to Boiling Springs PA. The day was filled with hiking thru PA game lands and farmers fields which was a big change in the scenery from the green tunnel. The day ended at Lisa’s Hostel outside of town after a small resupply. So at the hostel I had a hot shower which was great and was able to ordered a couple of appetizers, a Stromboli, and a few beers. That night those staying in the private trailer invited the bunk crew to their space to eat our dinners and watch a movie. The interesting thing was the owner showed up soon after we all got together to let the bunk crew know we WERE NOT ALLOWED TO USE THE BATHROOM IN THE TRAILER!!! We had to walk back to our space if we needed to go.
