I have a short hiking day to get into Waynesboro 5.7 miles. I woke-up at 4:45 had a good breakfast and was on trail by 5:45. I got to Rockfish Gap my pickup point at 7:10 and was in the shuttle by 7:30 heading to Stanimal’s Waynesboro Hostel. Once in town I was able to shower, do laundry, resupply for the Shenandoah section and eat a great lunch-IPA’s and pulled pork sandwich.
