In the morning Lisa drove us back to Boiling Springs at 730. Once in town I stopped at the cafe to have breakfast before heading back on the trail for a short 14+ mile day. They had the best stuffed french toast, bacon, orange juice and coffee. I headed out on the trail with a group of other hikers and we ended up at a diner near Carlisle PA around noon where I got second breakfast-blueberry pancakes with blueberry topping and whip cream!  Second Bkfst powered me thru the rest of the day to the Darlington shelter.
