We left Pine Ellis for another slack packing day from RT17 to RT4 13 miles stopping at the Hiker Hut Hostel-an off the grid place with no electricity but it did have a shower with a battery operated pump and propane heated on demand hot water. We had dinner in Rangeley ME where we could charge our electronics and also get ice cream. While at the Hostel we arranged two more days of slack packing that would get us to the Hostel of Maine.
