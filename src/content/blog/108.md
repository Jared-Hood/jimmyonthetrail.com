Todays hike was easy with the trail being mostly flat except one hill at the beginning. We headed to our midday stop at the cookie lady house which was a farm that offered lemon-aid and cookies on a donation basis. The place had a Hammock, benches, hanging chairs and a grassy yard where hikers could relax before continuing down the trail. The remainder of the day was a continuation of the flat trail.
