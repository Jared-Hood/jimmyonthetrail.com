We all left the Devils Backbone Campground and Brewery at 7:30. We got a hitch from camp back to Reed’s Gap to start my 14.2 mile trek to the Paul C. Wolf Shelter which was very nice with a porch covering the picnic table a running stream nearby for soaking my aching feet and a privy. The young crowd I was with continued on to town for the night while I had the shelter to myself with a few hikers staying in the tenting area.
