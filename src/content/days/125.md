I left the shelter knowing it was a short 15.5 mile day to a hostel to resupply for a 1 day hike to North Woodstock. Once at the Hostel we all took advantage of the shuttle into town for pizza, beer, snacks, ham and cheese sandwich and a parfait for dessert plus breakfast/lunch snacks for the 17th.

The one interesting point today was summiting Cube mountain looking South and seeing the fire tower I passed the previous day. It's amazing how far away it looks from my current position.
