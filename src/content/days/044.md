I left camp at 6:10 and hiked until 17:45 completing 21.3 miles setting up the opportunity to shorten my upcoming days as I make my way to resupply in Pearisburg Va. The hiking was mostly a ridge walk with about 4000ft of elevation change. There were some great views that I got pictures of and I saw a few deer that allowed me to get as close as 15 feet without running off- maybe I no longer smell like a human threat? I camped at a campsite close to I77 so I had truck noises throughout the night.
