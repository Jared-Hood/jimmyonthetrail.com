I left Daleville at 8:30 to hike for 18.5 miles to the Bobblets Gap Shelter. When I arrived all the tenting sites were taken with no opportunity to make a stealth site because of all the rocks in the area and no desire to hike back up hill to the AT to search for a stealth site so into the shelter I went. I had a quick dinner and straight to bed and the two Brits I met on trail showed up and had to jam into the shelter for the night.
