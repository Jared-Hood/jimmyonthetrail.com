Today we headed to the Clarendon Shelter which is near the Clarendon Gorge that was once a part of an ancient sea and you can find fossils of ancient sea creatures. When I passed over the gorge on the suspension bridge there were locals swimming in the stream and there was a guy panning for “GOLD”.

Now the last mile to the shelter was the hardest climb of the day it felt like it was straight up with a rock scramble-ugh!
