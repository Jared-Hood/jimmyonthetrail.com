Today I decided to do a shorter hike into Unionville to stay at the Sola hostel run by the pastor of the Presbyterian church. It was great to get a shower and wash my gear.

The town had a pizza shop and a tavern so I visited both to add much needed calories to my diet. Pizza, garlic knots and a soda from the pizza shop then a burger, salad and a few beers at the tavern.
