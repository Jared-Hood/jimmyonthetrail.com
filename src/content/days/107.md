Today's hike ended at possibly the best shelter on trail the Upper Goose Pond Shelter which is managed by a caretaker. The lake was beautiful and the shelter had 15 bunks with great mattresses and this day they had been given trail magic food: Oreo cookies, oranges, veggies, and Spaghetti all provided to the hikers free.
